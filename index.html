🥭 Papaya — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Papaya loyalty rewards app ("Papaya", "we", "us") collects and processes your personal data, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data controller

Partogi (sole operator of Papaya)
Contact: togijam@yahoo.com

2. What data we collect and why

DataPurposeLegal basis (Art. 6 GDPR)
Email address and password Creating and securing your account, login Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Display name Showing your profile in the app Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Loyalty activity (papayas earned and redeemed, shops visited, timestamps, XP level) Operating the loyalty program: tracking balances, rewards, and progress Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Shop details (for merchant accounts: shop name, rewards offered) Operating merchant features Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

3. Camera access

Papaya uses your device camera solely to scan QR codes for earning and redeeming rewards. No photos or video are recorded, stored, or transmitted.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a database operated by Supabase, our hosting provider. We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers. Data is shared with service providers only to the extent needed to operate the app (Supabase for database and authentication hosting; Apple for app distribution).

5. How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, or ask us to, your personal data is deleted. Anonymized transaction statistics may be retained.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • correct inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17)
  • restrict or object to processing (Art. 18, 21)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at togijam@yahoo.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Papaya is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, when new features are added). The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update shown above.

Papaya is currently in beta testing via Apple TestFlight. Beta builds may additionally send crash reports and usage diagnostics to Apple in accordance with Apple's TestFlight terms, which you accept when joining the beta.
